About Nova Aesthetic

Aesthetic lamps & night lights

About Us

Nova Aesthetic is a small online store for aesthetic lamps and night lights. We pick designs that make a room feel calm, warm and a little more special, from crystal ball lamps to moon, galaxy and Saturn lights.

We believe good lighting changes the mood of a space, and we want ordering to be simple and honest: clear prices, Cash on Delivery, and photos of the actual products.

Every order is checked and placed personally, and if anything goes wrong you can message us directly.

Delivery

  • We deliver across India.
  • Delivery usually takes 5-8 days after your order is confirmed.
  • Payment is Cash on Delivery. Please keep the exact amount ready.
  • We may contact you on the mobile number you give to confirm your order or address.

Returns & Replacements

You can ask for a replacement or refund within 3 days of delivery if:

  • the lamp arrives damaged or broken,
  • the lamp is defective or not working, or
  • you receive a different product from what you ordered.

How to request: please record a clear unboxing video while opening the parcel, then message us (Instagram DM or the form below) with your name, order details and the video or photos.

Used items, or items damaged after delivery, cannot be returned. Approved refunds are sent within 7-10 working days.
